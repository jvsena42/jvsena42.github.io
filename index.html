Our apps

Two products, one principle

Both ship for phones and tablets, both keep their state on hardware or infrastructure the person using them owns.

Loopky
Learn anything, remember everything
Android & iOS

A flashcards app that fuses TinyCards' playfulness, Anki's spaced repetition, and Pubky's decentralized identity and social graph. Study queues are scheduled for you; the grade buttons tell you the interval before you press them.

There is no Loopky account and no Loopky server. You sign in with a key held by Pubky Ring, and your decks and study progress are written to a Pubky homeserver you hold the key to. Built with Kotlin Multiplatform, with native SwiftUI screens on iOS and real two-pane layouts on tablets.

  • Spaced repetition
  • Paste-to-import decks
  • Pubky identity
  • Deck discovery
  • Tablet layouts

Mandacaru 🌵
Your mobile Bitcoin node
Android

A lightweight Bitcoin validator node that runs on your phone, powered by Utreexo and Floresta. Utreexo's accumulator replaces the full UTXO set with a compact proof, which brings storage down from tens of gigabytes to something a handset can carry — so the validating is yours, not a server's.

It ships a block and transaction explorer, raw transaction broadcast, peer controls, and a built-in Electrum server you can pair your own wallet with. Mainnet, Testnet, Testnet4, Signet and Regtest, in a Material 3 interface with light and dark themes.

  • Utreexo
  • Floresta
  • Electrum server
  • Block explorer
  • Multi-network

About

Who is behind Bullet Tech

Bullet Tech is the independent studio of João Victor Sena, a mobile developer working on Android, iOS and Kotlin Multiplatform. It exists to ship a small number of apps properly rather than many quickly.

Privacy by architecture

We prefer designs where there is no central database to leak: keys on the device, state on infrastructure the user controls, no accounts to create.

Open by default

Our apps are developed in public on GitHub. Anyone can read what the app actually does instead of taking a marketing page's word for it.

Native craft

Jetpack Compose on Android, SwiftUI on iOS, shared logic in Kotlin Multiplatform and Rust. Adaptive layouts for phones and tablets, not stretched columns.

Privacy

What we collect

Bullet Tech does not operate accounts, profiles or analytics backends for the apps listed on this page, and this website itself sets no cookies and embeds no trackers. Each app states its own data handling in full; those statements are the authoritative policies.
